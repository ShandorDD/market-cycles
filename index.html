The current bull market began at the October 2022 trough — the deepest bear since 2011 was shallow by historical standards, and the rally that followed has outlasted every bull market since 1987. History offers no precedent for how this one ends. It may yet produce a 20% drawdown and become the next entry in the table above.

Method. A bear market is a decline of 20% or more from a running peak. An interim rally only ends a bear when the index closes back above that bear's starting peak; otherwise the bear extends. That rule merges the two-wave 2000–02 and 2007–09 declines into one bear each. The bull market that follows runs from the bear's trough to the next peak. Figures are computed from daily closing prices, not from published summaries.
